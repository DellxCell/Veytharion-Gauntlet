Veytharion's Gauntlet1.2.1

A boss arena Survival mode for Black Ops 7 Zombies on Rex Infernus, made for Kronos. Jump to any boss round, beat each boss for an upgrade that lasts the match, and take on all four at round 999.

New in 1.2.1

Wonderbar, salvage, a tougher Caltheris and fixes

  • Wonderbar loves the BlightWonderbar gives the Warden Blight about half the time, and never a second one if you already hold it.
  • 5,000 salvage per bossEvery player gets 5,000 salvage each time a boss is beaten, in every boss round.
  • A tougher CaltherisHalf the damage in phase 2 and a quarter in phase 3, for every weapon, even one picked up mid-fight.
  • FixesA player leaving a squad leaves no toy glows behind, the Warden end works with no players left, and old test code is gone.

Boss rounds

Play up to them or jump there from a pedestal

  • Round 50: Z-RexGolden Armor for every player.
  • Round 75: The GuardianUltra weapon rarity, also added to the Arsenal.
  • Round 100: CaltherisPack-a-Punch IV.
  • Round 255 and 500Z-Rex and the Guardian, then the three of them together.
  • Round 999: The WardenAll four bosses. The Warden fakes its death twice, then BOSSES DEFEATED and the Continue Playing? vote.
  • Boss Rush styleA prepare timer before each fight, a health bar per boss, Max Ammo, Full Power, a Free Perk and Max Armor from every boss, and the round ends when the last boss dies.

In the arena

Rex Infernus' copy of the Ashes boss arena

  • Round-select pedestalsSix pedestals with Rex's Easter egg toys. Walk up and they glow; press Use to go to that round.
  • Void Claw and Void TalonThree Void Claw pedestals. Beat Z-Rex, the Guardian and Caltheris and it becomes the Void Talon, unlimited for the match.
  • Drop-inYou drop into the arena from above, like the ride down from Tyr in Totenreich.
  • Clean screenNo minimap or objective tracker, and a custom loading intro of the arena.

Get started

  1. Set up Kronos and run its launcher at least once.
  2. Download Veytharions-Gauntlet-1.2.1.zip from the latest release.
  3. Close Black Ops 7 and KronosLauncher, then extract the zip into C:\Kronos\cod25\kronos\mods. You should end up with mods\rex_arena\mod.json.
  4. Launch through Kronos and pick Zombies > Survival > Veytharion's Gauntlet.

Good to know

  • Avoid Save & Quit in the gauntlet; saving and resuming in the arena hasn't been tested.
  • With Carry-forward on in KronosLauncher, some launches start badly and the drop-in can freeze. Restart the game through Kronos.
  • Beaten bosses' bodies stay in the arena for the rest of the match.
  • Squad play hasn't been fully tested yet.
